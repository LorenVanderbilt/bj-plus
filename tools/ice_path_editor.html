Jungle Zone 2: ice path editor

Drag the ice blocks and the house. Double-click empty space to add a block; select one and press Delete (or right-click it) to remove it. Positions snap to 2 px, as in the game. The view is the game's highest camera (as when Blue is up at the house).


Blocks hidden below the bottom of this view (lower than the screen) can't be shown; keep the path within the picture. The shaded strip on the right is the next block of the level (blocks can't go there). The dotted boxes are the HUD - the hearts, score and time are drawn over anything there in the game.
